- When two or more than two devices are connected for information interchange is known as a network. 
- They way two or more than two devices are connected is known as physical topology.
- When two or more than two networks are connected together is known as inter-networking or internet. 
- The biggest internet is Internet. 

## Types of Networks Size
###  LAN - Local Area Network :
A network < 2km approx. , e.g. College, campus, a building 
### MAN - Metropolitan Area Network : 
A network in between 2 to 10 km approx. , e.g. Regional Media channels 
### WAN - Wide Area Network: 
A network > 10 km approx. , e.g. www • 
### PAN - Personal Area Network: 
A network used in home, e.g. home network 

## Accessibility 
• Public Network - The network which is available publicly and can be accessed by anyone in the internet 
• Private Network - The network which is not available publicly and need proper path and credentials to access the network
